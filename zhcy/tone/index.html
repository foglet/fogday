    Tone Pairs

    We present forty-one tone pairs. There are a minimum of fifteen voices for each. Six are Taiwanese; the remainder are from Mainland China.

    The grid is random because we hear sounds randomly. There are two words for every tone pair. Master these… no more culinary surprises.

    For 自摸 zìmō, we list sixty ways to say it, including six from Hong Kong. Mahjong forever.

    Mandarin has four tones and a light, neutral tone. Many everyday words have two syllables, so tones are easiest to learn in pairs: how the first tone flows into the second.

    Tone sandhi: when two third tones meet, the first is spoken as a second tone. 雨伞 is written yǔsǎn but said yú sǎn; red lines under such a word show how it is spoken.

    Tap a word to hear it, and tap again for another voice. The voices are made with ondoku3.com text to speech. Swipe or use the arrow keys for the next word. The game controller shows every word in a grid; 發財 brings back one word at a time.
