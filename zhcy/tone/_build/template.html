    Tone Pairs

    A tone pair is two tones in a row. In 美国 měiguó, měi is third tone and guó is second, so it’s a 3-2 pair. Most everyday Mandarin words have two syllables, and speech tends to move in two-syllable chunks. There are twenty tone pairs. We give you two of each tone pair, and fifteen voices for each, from Mainland China and Taiwan. We used ondoku3.com.

    Natural speech follows this rhythm. Learn these and thrive.

    Bonus: 自摸 zìmō has sixty voices. Mahjong forever.
