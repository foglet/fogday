    Tap a route, in the legend or on the map, to highlight it; tap it again to show all. Tap a shuttle for its next stop, or a stop for its next departures. With location allowed, the map finds you and Info lists the stops nearest you; nothing is kept. On iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, on, with Safari Websites set to While Using.

    Weekday service only. Schedule positions don't know about delays, detours or cancelled runs; the official tracker is liveshuttle.ucsf.edu. Not affiliated with UCSF.
