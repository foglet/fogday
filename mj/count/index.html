Terminology

A winning Mahjong hand is made up of four sets (or melds) and a pair. Sets can be a sequence, triplet, or quadruplet.

Sequence: Three consecutive tiles of the same suit, from 1-2-3 through 7-8-9. Complete a sequence from the player to your left by calling a Chow or Seung.

Triplet: Three identical tiles. Complete one from any player’s discard as a Pong.

Quadruplet: Four identical tiles. Complete one from any player’s discard as a Kong, or declare a Concealed Kong if self-formed. When declaring, draw a replacement tile from the dead end of the wall to maintain the correct tile count.

A winning hand can be called for Mahjong, but its worth depends on its fan. Hong Kong style often uses a minimum fan: without enough fan, you cannot declare a win.
