# fogday.com/qr: Pixel QR

Random lo-res pixel QR codes, Website menu shows names only: Fogday Studios (default), Ohlone Sky, Oyster Atlas, Instagram (instagram.com/fogfish, shown in the website box as @fogfish), WhatsApp (wa.me/14158733873?text=Bon%20jour%21, shown as 4158733873), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/fogfish). Under More, a box shows the address in use without https:// (fogday.com) and can be edited to override the menu: a typed website that is not on the menu brings a dragon into the menu box: it walks back and forth and breathes fire or water (about 6.5 s), then flies out into the page (`px:spawn` to the front layer, which keeps the group at four) and the menu shows "Your website". Picking "Your website" from the menu opens More and puts the cursor in the address box; clearing the box goes back to Fogday Studios. The menu always starts on Fogday Studios (no saved choice); a shared link with `&u=` keeps its website. Default caption "Toï Toï Toï !!" (the pixel font has Ï, Ë, Ä, Ö, Ü with dots two rows above); once edited, yours is kept, blank included (`qr:cap`). Remix gives a new one; every seed is a real, different code.

Pixel level: Pixel Waves v9 (chunky hard pixels, integer colour steps, the picture about 90 to 130 px wide). 3 px per module for short addresses; long ones drop to 2 or 1 px per module, with flat or dither shapes and square or bevel corners.

- Edit `template.html`, never `../index.html`, then run `python3 build.py` (it inlines `sprites.json`: the /px dragons, flying frames 8 and 9).
- Encoder: byte mode, versions 1 to 40, levels L/M/Q/H, any mask. Bit-exact with python `qrcode` (all 560 combos for the fogday URLs, plus samples of every version 1 to 40).
- Each remix picks: URL with or without trailing slash, level M/Q/H, version (minimum or one up), mask 0 to 7, palette, module shape (flat, brick, gem, dither, round), finder (square, round, eye), frame (bands, checker, stars, plain).
- Scan margins: dark modules at or under 0.10 luminance, light at or over 0.74, 4-module quiet zone.
- No frame: past the quiet zone the code's own data texture drifts outward (wind direction, trails, value-noise clumps) and fades away over 4 to 6 modules (7 to 9 with a picture). Tested: 400 seeds (200 plain, 200 with a picture) all decode in zbar at 3x, 4x, 6x and blurred 2x.
- Pictures: the Add picture and Remove picture buttons were removed at the owner's request (the background-picture code is still in render() if wanted again).
- Text me: share sheet with the PNG (Web Share with files); where that is missing it saves the PNG to attach by hand.
- Caption: 3x5 pixel font, 22 chars, saved under `qr:caption`. URL hash holds the seed (`#572228`).
- Styles: add an entry to `STYLES`; the Style picker shows once there are two.
- Corner markers: random on every remix: square, round, eye, dot, bevel. Circle and morph were dropped: morph failed about 1 code in 10 in zbar. Check: 560 codes (7 websites incl. a long one, 40 seeds, with and without a picture, default caption) all decode in zbar at 2x, 3x and 4x.
- Link format: `#SEED`, plus `&u=site` when not fogday.com.
- Layout: [3 px][中][New scene]; [caption | Text me] (the box you type in widens, then shrinks back); website menu; website box. No More toggle. While the website box has focus the menu shows — (the owner's choice; this is the one dash the UI-text check flags). New scene also remixes, in the current mode, like 3 px or 中. The mahjong button shows 中發白, the three dragon tiles' faces (the same 10x12 glyphs as the tiles on the code) drawn in 2 px squares: red 中, green 發, blue-framed 白 (inline SVG). In 中 the caption sits inside the bottom margin so the code stays square.
- Minecraft look: dark modules are textured blocks from 13 coloured materials (crimson stem, purpur, amethyst, leaves, oxidized copper, dark oak and spruce logs, nether brick, dark prismarine, lapis, redstone, warped stem, mud). Never black: no grey or black materials, and the shadow edge keeps the hue, sometimes two mixed, corner markers in a third; lit north edge, shadowed south edge, a thin cast shadow on the ground below; ore specks (diamond, emerald, gold, redstone, lapis, copper) never on a module centre. Ground is snow, quartz, concrete, calcite, end stone or bone. Caption letters are textured in the code's own block material, on the light ground. Loose blocks past the quiet zone are solid (no fade).
- Walking dragon: one /px dragon (walking frames, random set and colours per code) walks round the code with its feet on the edge of the quiet zone, turning at each corner (standing on top, down the right side, upside down along the bottom, up the left). The margin widens to fit it and the caption gets its own rows below. It is in the PNG that Text me sends. Tested: 240 frames across 30 codes all decode in zbar at 2x, 3x and 5x. Reduced motion: it stands still.
- Background: /px in two layers sharing one landscape seed. Back (`/px/?bg&seed=`): the landscape only, behind the page. Front (`/px/?fg&seed=`): up to four dragons (no babies, 0.5x) and the weather, see-through, over the fields and buttons, never taking taps; the code sits above it so it always scans. Both follow /px live (storage event; seed derived from the saved settings). New scene: the back layer rolls a landscape and posts it to /qr, which hands it to the front layer (`px:apply`) with new dragons. Neither layer saves to the visitor's /px settings.
- Tap the page (anywhere that is not a control or the code) and the front-layer dragons shoot fire or water at it, as on /px; hold 2.5 s and the flames stop and they follow your finger. /qr forwards pointer events to the front layer (`px:ptr` down, move, up); /px's own handlers were split into pDown and pMove for this.
- Every button, the code details, the website menu, the caption box (placeholder too) and the address box take the pattern of the last dragon that flew over it (its body, wing and belly colours as a 6x6 pixel scale tile, 50% see-through) until the next one does; the page title and the projects icon take its colours as a gradient. The front /px layer posts dragon positions and colours (`px:where`, every 120 ms).
- Code details: two lines, "No. N · version V" and "level L · mask M".
- Burning: fire or water from any dragon (the walker's own breath, or the /px dragons' breath reported in `px:where` hits) burns the loose blocks past the quiet zone: flames, then grey, then gone (water skips the flames). The code itself never burns. New scene puts every block back and brings the walker back; the website stays.
- The walker may walk off the code some time in its first minute and join the dragons over the page (`px:spawn`).
- Remix has the same light 50% style as the other buttons. When a dragon repaints a button, its text takes one of that dragon's colours that contrasts 4.5:1 or more with the pattern (else ink or white), with a thin opposite shadow.
- Focus: a field (website menu, caption, address box) goes solid while it has focus, and back to 50% see-through after.
- Opacity: everything (buttons, menu, fields, the details chip) is 50% see-through; only the menu or a box with focus is solid (in the body colour of the last dragon that passed over it, with ink or white text; paper if none has), and after 2 s with no typing, tapping or change in it, it lets go (blurs) and goes back. Repainted text is chosen to read on each pattern colour on its own.
- No borders on any box or button; keyboard focus still shows the red ring.
- Calm: on load, Remix and New scene every box goes back to plain 50% (dragon colours cleared) and the dragons cannot repaint anything for 5 s.
- Size: one size whatever Remix makes. Phone (up to 600px wide): the code is 95% of the screen width. Wider: it fills the room left after the header, the details and the controls (More not counted). Drawn nearest-neighbour on device pixels. Always the smallest code for the address and a 5-module margin (plus the walker's lane). Buttons, menu and boxes are 40px tall; nothing scrolls with More closed on a 390x664 phone view.
- Full screen: the corner button (or F) where the browser allows it; iPhone: Add to Home Screen (`manifest.webmanifest`, display fullscreen, /px icons). The status line floats at the bottom so it never takes room.
- Quiet time: for 6 s after any tap, key or typing (and after load) the code is a no-fly zone: the front /px dragons are pushed out of its area (`px:nofly` with the code's rectangle, every 250 ms) and the walker is away (not drawn, no breath). After 6 quiet seconds the walker is back, dragons may cross, and a dragon flying over the code changes its ground to its own colours, lifted to luminance 0.76 or more so it still scans (tested on three palettes at 2x, 3x and 5x). Remix, a new website or caption, and New scene put the ground back.
- Idle (10 s with no tap, key or typing): every box, button, the menu, the details and the code's light ground go fully transparent (light pixels, luminance over about 0.55, get alpha 0; the dark squares, walker, fire and burns stay). Words get a soft light halo. Any tap brings everything back at once (for scanning). Text me always sends the solid image (tapping it is activity).
- Never the same pattern: every seed shown is remembered on the device (`qr:seen`, up to 20,000) and never picked again. Changing the website is a Remix (new seed).
- Twinkle: going from one code to the next, every pixel changes at its own moment over 0.9 s and flashes like a star as it turns (gold on light, white on dark); the old code is stretched onto the new grid when sizes differ. Reduced motion: instant.
- 中 (mahjong), made like fogday.com/mj: the "3 px" and "中" buttons replace Remix; each switches mode on the first tap (new code) and remixes on the next (`&m=mj` in the link). 中 is level H, version 6 or more, 12 art px per square: dark squares are Morse dots and dashes (each run along a row one bar, half a square tall) in /mahjong green on ivory; timing lines and the alignment marker solid; corner markers are /mj's cookies (green ring, green disc, red centre). About N²/100 small tiles (12x16, symbols from /mahjong's scatter_mahjong_qr.py) lie on the code at random angles, never on the corner, timing or alignment markers; every 1.1 s one flips (green back) to a different tile; a few lie face down. The walking dragon keeps its 3 px look (4 art px per dragon pixel). Default caption 自摸 in tile red; 3 px default stays "Toï Toï Toï !!"; a caption you type is kept for both. Checked: 150 中 codes with their tiles (6 websites x 25) decode at 3 sizes.
- Caption box and website box: tapping in clears it for fresh typing; leaving without typing puts the old text back (the code does not change).

- Header: no visible title or Projects (bento) icon; "Pixel QR" stays as a screen-reader-only h1. The full-screen button sits at the right end of the No./version line. The website box text is 15 px, like the buttons and menu.
- 中 mode: the pattern drifts past the quiet zone as in 3 px (Morse dots and dashes), and loose tiles lie scattered across the margin (about one per 100 margin squares), never in the quiet zone or on the caption. The canvas is 95% of the phone's width in both modes.
- Caption box: stays open (wide, solid) for 15 s without typing before it lets go; other boxes and the menu still let go after 2 s.
- A box or menu in use shoos the dragons: while it has focus, a no-fly zone 40 px around it keeps the /px front-layer dragons off (`px:nofly` now takes `rects`, a list; `rect` still works), and the menu's own dragon flies out at once.
- The website box stays empty while a menu website is in use (the menu names it; the code is the same); it shows only an address you typed. Typing @fogfish or 4158733873 still picks Instagram or WhatsApp.
- Rows: [No./version text | New scene | full screen]; [3 px | 中發白 | Reset]; [caption | Text me]; [website menu | website box]. The menu takes about 60% at rest and widens when focused. The box widens while you type (and stays wide for a long address, about 1 to 3.5 times the Clear button), and shrinks back when short or empty. Clear sends every dragon away for six seconds: the flying layer fades out and its fire, water, taps and recolouring are ignored, the walker steps off, and the menu dragon goes.
- 中 default caption: 自摸 (zì mō). The pixel font gained Ì (grave), Ō (macron) and parentheses.
- Reset (was Clear): website back to fogday.com (Fogday Studios, empty box, new pattern), caption back to the mode's default (Toï Toï Toï !! or 自摸 (zì mō)) and no longer saved, and every dragon away for six seconds.
- Focus anywhere (any button, box or menu) sends every dragon away for six seconds, as Reset does; each new focus restarts the six seconds. Taps on the open page (no focus) still make the dragons shoot.
- Menu captions: picking a menu website sets the caption (and forgets a typed one): Ohlone Sky "Bay to Sky!", Oyster Atlas "An oyster a day...", Instagram "Beach time!", WhatsApp "Wesh, ça va ?", LinkedIn "Let's connect!"; Fogday Studios and typed websites use the mode default. Reset, a mode switch and reload follow the same rule. (The pixel font already had an apostrophe.)
- The pixel font has Ç with the cedilla hanging two rows below the line.
